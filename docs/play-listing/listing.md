## Full description (4,000 at most)

One letter. Four answers. How fast can you write them?

Fast Four is the classic word game also known as Name, Place, Animal, Thing. Each round a random letter is tossed and you have 60 seconds to write a Name, a Place, an Animal and a Thing that start with it.

PLAY YOUR WAY

- Solo: race the clock over 5, 10, 15 or 20 rounds and chase your best score.
- Live groups: create a room, share the 6-character code and play with 2 to 8 friends in real time.

FAIR AND FAST SCORING

- A unique answer scores 10 points, a shared answer scores 5.
- Be the first to finish with four valid answers for a bonus.
- Think a word is wrong? Challenge it. Think yours was unfairly rejected? Appeal it. The result is checked once and is final.

MADE FOR FRIENDS

- Live scores after every round, final standings and leaderboards.
- Chat in the room, with report and block tools to keep it friendly.
- Light and dark themes.

Fast Four is free, has no ads and asks for no permissions other than internet access. For players aged 13 and over.
